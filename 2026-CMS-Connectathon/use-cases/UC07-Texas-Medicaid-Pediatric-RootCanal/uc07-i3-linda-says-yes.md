**Corresponds to:** 2026-07-22, 09:30 (consultation) through approximately 10:00 onward (treatment completed).

## What happens

A week after approval, Joe arrives at Dr. Garcia's office with his mother, **Linda Castle**. Dr. Garcia already has everything Dr. Parker sent, so he does not start from a blank chart. He reviews the records and radiographs, confirms the diagnosis independently, and goes over Joe's medical history and current medications for any surgical risk.

While he does, his staff run an **eligibility check** with DentaQuest to confirm Joe's coverage is still active and to see what benefits have been used to date. The authorization from a week ago does not guarantee that nothing has changed.

Joe is 16, so **consent comes from his guardian**. Dr. Garcia explains the risks and benefits of root canal therapy, build-up, and crown to Joe and Linda together, and Linda provides consent on Joe's behalf. Dr. Garcia then completes all three procedures on tooth #4.

## Why this matters for testing

- **Re-verification at the time of service.** Does the practice's system run a fresh 270/271 rather than trust the PA from a week earlier? Coverage can lapse, and Medicaid eligibility can change in a week.
- **Guardian consent for a minor.** Joe is the patient and Linda is the consenting party. The test is whether `Consent` correctly names a `RelatedPerson` as the actor and ties the consent to the specific authorized procedures, rather than to Joe himself.
- **Bundled treatment captured accurately.** Three CDT-coded procedures on one tooth, in one visit, each recorded individually and each traceable to the PA number from Interaction 2.
