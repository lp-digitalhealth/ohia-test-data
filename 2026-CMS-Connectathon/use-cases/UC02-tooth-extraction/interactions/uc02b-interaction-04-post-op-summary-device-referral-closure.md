# UC02b — Interaction 4: Post-Operative Summary & Referral Closure (with Device Record)

**Corresponds to:** 2026-07-15, 11:45–11:46 (same day as the procedure).

## What happens

Dr. Maxil's system sends Frank's structured summary back to Dr. Parker's practice — what was extracted, what was placed, how it went. Critically, the implant `Device` record travels with it: the exact specifications Dr. Parker's practice will need months from now, when Frank comes back for the crown, without anyone having to call Austin Oral Surgery and ask what, exactly, got placed.

## What Frank Sees (patient-facing)

📱 **Notification:** *"Your referral is complete — summary sent to Dr. Parker."*

## Why this matters for testing

This is the actual payoff of Interaction 3's `Device` work — not just documenting the implant, but proving it's genuinely *useful* once it's somewhere else. The test: can Dr. Parker's practice, four months from now, pull up the exact implant specifications without a records request, and pick the correct abutment and crown components on the first try. That's a real, practical interoperability win this project can point to concretely, not just a structural exercise.

## What's deliberately NOT part of this interaction

Getting Dr. Maxil's practice paid for the extraction and implant — that's Interaction 5. Also not part of this: the actual crown placement itself, which happens months later at Dr. Parker's practice and is outside this use case's scope entirely.
