# UC02a — Interaction 4: Post-Operative Summary & Referral Closure

**Corresponds to:** 2026-07-21, 11:30–11:31 (same day as the surgery).

## What happens

Later that same morning — by 11:30 — Dr. Maxil's system sends a structured summary directly back to Dr. Parker's practice: what was done, how it went, and what Frank needs going forward. Not a scanned note, not a fax — the same kind of structured push that brought the referral to Dr. Maxil in the first place, now running in reverse.

## What Frank Sees (patient-facing)

📱 **Notification:** *"Your referral is complete — summary sent to Dr. Parker."*

The referral that opened back in Interaction 2 formally closes. Dr. Parker's practice now has a complete record of what happened at the surgical visit, available the moment Frank calls or walks back in for anything related to that tooth — no records request, no waiting on a fax that never quite arrives.

## Why this matters for testing

This is structurally the same pattern already proven valuable in UC01's clearance interaction: the moment one real-world event (the summary being sent) closes out the loop on the receiving end. The test here is whether that same closed-loop pattern — proven once already in a medical-to-dental context — holds up cleanly in a pure dental-to-dental referral too, without needing to be redesigned for this direction.

## What's deliberately NOT part of this interaction

Getting Dr. Maxil's practice actually paid for the extraction — that's Interaction 5.
